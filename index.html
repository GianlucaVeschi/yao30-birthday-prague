About this experience

Gianluca turns 30 on November 2nd, but proper celebrations don't happen on a Tuesday. They happen on a long weekend in Prague, surrounded by people who actually matter. This is a curated 3-day experience covering accommodation, activities, and whatever moderately questionable decisions follow.

Accommodation is fully host-paid. All scheduled activities are covered. You are responsible for getting yourself to Prague and feeding yourself between planned meals. Everything else is handled.

No dress code. No agenda beyond the one below. No refunds. No regrets. Probably.

What you'll do

a

Friday · Nov 6 — Evening

Arrival & Pork Dinner

Gather at the Airbnb, settle in, argue about beds. Then: dinner. Proper Prague roasted pork at a place that takes it seriously. Golden, crispy, unapologetic. The only reasonable way to begin a birthday weekend.

b

Friday · Nov 6 — Night

Kafka Castle Visit

Gianluca has a Kafka tattoo. He will tell you he knows a lot about Kafka. He does not. But the castle is genuinely stunning at night, and the walk through Prague's old town is worth the mild existential dread.

c

Saturday · Nov 7 — Morning

Acroyoga & Handstand Workshop

Morning movement. Getting upside down together. No prior experience necessary — only a tolerance for mild embarrassment and a willingness to trust someone else's feet beneath your hips. Good for the hangover.

d

Saturday · Nov 7 — Evening

Moscow Mule & Ketamine Tour

Ketamine therapy is the fastest-growing mental health treatment in the world. We're getting ahead of the curve. The cocktails are for set and setting. The shaman is Gianluca. The therapy is unsupervised. Results guaranteed.

e

Sunday · Nov 8 — Night

The Kinky Night Out

An underground dungeon. Candlelight. Prague's finest subterranean experience. Nobody knows the exact location. Nobody is officially in charge. That is the point. This one is complimentary — some things shouldn't have a price.

f

Sunday · Nov 8 — Afternoon

Tram Ride of Fools

Tram #58. Terminus. Return. No plan, no guide, no destination beyond the end of the line. Prague happens around you. The perfect ending to an imperfect weekend. Possibly the most meaningful tram ride you'll ever take.
